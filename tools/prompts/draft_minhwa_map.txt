Small game map piece for a 2D story learning game set in late-Goryeo Songdo (Gaegyeong), Korea: the same walled garden of a noble family seen from directly above, in a top-down 2D RPG map view (orthographic, looking straight down, with only a slight hint of the tops of roofs and trees), square format.

Contents: a garden wall of earth and stone with a tiled coping forming the border of the garden along two sides of the square; flowering trees (peach, apricot, plum) along the inside of the wall, their pink and white blossoms seen from above; a small wooden pavilion seen from above as a small tiled roof square; neat flower beds with stone edging; a garden swing hanging between two posts; a narrow earth path that winds through the garden; a small pond with stepping stones. Outside the wall, in one corner, a large weeping willow by a dirt lane. The scale is like a game map where small character sprites would walk: each character would be about one tile wide (one twentieth of the image width). Place two tiny character figures at that sprite scale, seen from above at a slight angle: a young woman in a soft-colored long skirt near the pavilion inside the wall, and a young scholar in a light robe and a black cap under the willow outside the wall. Clear readable shapes that could be cut into map tiles, even soft lighting from the top-left, no perspective distortion. Spring, peaceful. No fire, no smoke.

Art style: Korean folk painting (minhwa) combined with a hand-colored woodblock print look, like late-Joseon printed storybook illustrations: bold carved black outlines with the slight irregularity of a carved wood block, flat areas of bright but soft mineral colors (vermilion red, indigo blue, ochre yellow, malachite green, peach pink), decorative stylized flowers, clouds, rocks and willow leaves, slightly naive charming figures, visible wood grain and ink-press texture on cream mulberry (hanji) paper. Strong graphic readability. Absolutely no text, no letters, no written characters, no calligraphy, no poem inscription, no seals, no stamps, no signatures, no captions, no watermarks, no frames or borders, no UI, no grid lines.
